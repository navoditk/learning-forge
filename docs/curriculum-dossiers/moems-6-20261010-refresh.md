  (CCSS-M). MOEMS stays additive contest preparation.

  Division E _or_ Division M (unchanged rule, reverified below); this refresh
  does **not** switch the learner to Division M or dual placement.

- **Out of scope** (unchanged): Division M, APSMO (Junior/Senior/"Division S"),

## Domain coverage, sequencing, tiers (delta only)

- **Coverage signal**: R7 is the closest official topic document located.
  It supports Division E families already in the baseline: whole-number and
  place-value reasoning, digit and number properties, divisibility and
  factors, primes, GCF/LCM, fractions and decimals, mean, median, mode and
  simple probability, plane geometry with perimeter and area, measurement and
  conversions within a system, and strategy use. R7 is a vocabulary and
  conventions guide, not a syllabus or weighting. Authoring must still
  cross-check representative archive years (R11) before treating coverage as
  complete.
- **Division E boundary (optional contest preparation only)**: the
  recommendation is that Division E contest-tier items not _require_, as an
  unstated prerequisite, any topic R7 labels Division M:
  - square root, cube, perfect cube, or power/root relations;
  - negative fractions or signed-number operations;
  - percent;
  - the solids terms "Geometric Solid, Right Circular Cylinder, face, edge";
  - cubic measure or volume units.

  This applies unless the item defines the topic, matching R4's "include all
  necessary definitions." The guidance does not remove or reclassify any
  Grade 6 core (CCSS-M) coverage (C4).

- **Difficulty**: R8 suggests a typical Division E contest is hard for the
  median participant: about 10/25 overall, or roughly 2 of 5 per contest. A
  top-10% learner scored about 18–22/25. Use R8 only as a coarse band target.
  It does not validate item difficulty.
- **Sequencing**: unchanged. No district pacing applies. Use the repository
  prerequisite graph.
- **Tiers and answer conventions**: unchanged two tiers. R7 adds checkable
  answer conventions (equivalent forms accepted; units optional but must be
  correct if given; "or" is inclusive). Contest-tier items may mirror these
  conventions in original wording.
