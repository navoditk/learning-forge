## Standards-domain impact for the Grade 6 learner

No standards change. The MAA topic list is unchanged, so the baseline's domain
summary and the shipped Grade 6 CCSS-M mapping need no delta. The only
learner-facing impacts are: (a) AMC 8 is administered once a year in a January
window via a school/institutional competition manager, so any parent-facing
mention of "taking AMC 8" must be framed as optional and outside the product;
and (b) eligibility wording should read "grade 8 and below and under 15.5 on
competition day". Eligibility is not readiness: the existing core-prep-first,
evidence-gated contest tier is unaffected.

## Licensing and originality
