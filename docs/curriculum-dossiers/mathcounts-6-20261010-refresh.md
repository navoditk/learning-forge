## MATHCOUNTS Grade 6 source refresh — 2026-10-10

**Status: Approved by product/content owner on 2026-10-10 (source refresh only).**
Owner decision: "math counts is good as well", following independent review
of the corrected refresh. It does not re-scope or authorize any new authoring. The
approved baseline, _MATHCOUNTS Grade 6 research — 2026-09-18_ in
`docs/curriculum-sources.md`, retains its program boundaries alongside this
approved source-currency delta. Its core framework (scope, round table, scoring, topic

### Scope of this refresh

- **Learner**: same full-time Grade 6 learner as the baseline. No learner,
  school, or chapter identity was searched or recorded.
- **Season**: the current official season is **2026-2027**. The rules page
  names "2026-2027 Official Rules + Procedures" in its body.
- **Programs kept separate**:
  - **In scope**: MATHCOUNTS **Competition Series** (grades 6-8).
  - **Out of scope, separate program**: **The National Math Club**. The club
    page calls it non-competitive and free for groups of at least four U.S.
    students in grades 6-8. It is not contest preparation evidence.
  - **Out of scope, no longer standalone**: **Math Video Challenge**. Its
    page says that starting in 2023 it is no longer a standalone program and
    became a video project option inside the National Math Club.

    program**, which is in its Phase 2 pilot for 2026-27. The rules and FAQ
    both say it does not change the grades 6-8 Competition Series.
  - **Resources, not programs**: the School Handbook, Past Competitions,
    OPLET, Problem of the Week, MATHCOUNTS Minis, and Practice Plans.
- **Retrieval**: all checks ran on 2026-10-10 (America/Los_Angeles) with
  `curl -L`. HTTP codes below are the observed final status. Response
  `Last-Modified` headers on HTML pages showed 2026-10-10 or 2026-10-11 GMT.
  Those are render or transport times, not publication dates. PDF dates
  come from each document's internal `CreationDate`/`ModDate` and its printed

### Season, eligibility, and format summary for a Grade 6 learner

The learner is eligible as a full-time Grade 6 student through the official
school of record, or as an NSC only if that school will not register or
support participation. Grade 6 participation counts toward the 3-year
competition limit. Only the official school team of up to 4 takes the Team
Round, and NSCs cannot be team members. The current-season dates are those in
delta rows 2, 3, and 6. Round formats and scoring are unchanged from the
baseline (row 7). They are official contest-format facts that may inform
original practice-mode timing and calculator labels. They are not
pedagogical rules, mastery thresholds, or unlock criteria. Any new numeric
product policy needs a separate owner decision.

### Licence and originality bounds

The _Terms of Use_ prohibits reproduction without prior written consent.
Every handbook and competition PDF checked prints "All rights reserved." No
licence or consent granting Learning Forge reuse was located, so
reuse permission is **none**. The Competition Series page itself shows sample
problems with answers. Those, like all archive, handbook, Minis, Problem of
the Week, Practice Plan, OPLET, and grades 3-5 pilot items, must not be copied,
closely paraphrased, re-valued, or screenshotted. The current 2027 School
Competition must not be obtained, used, or referenced, because of the
confidentiality rule. Official materials may inform only broad skill types,
topic coverage, relative difficulty, calculator posture, round timing, and
mechanics, as the baseline already states. The _Terms of Use_ restriction on
names and marks has two branches. MATHCOUNTS' names, trademarks, service
marks and logos on the site may not be used in any advertising or publicity.
They also may not be used otherwise to indicate MATHCOUNTS' sponsorship of or
affiliation with any product or service. Either use needs MATHCOUNTS' prior
express written permission. No such permission exists, so Learning Forge
advertising and publicity must not use those names or marks, and the product
must not imply sponsorship or affiliation. Some uses fall outside advertising
or publicity: descriptive in-product mentions, and the repository-internal
identifiers `mathcounts-6`, `mc6-` and `MC6-`. Whether those uses are
permissible is an unresolved legal/owner question. This dossier does not
claim that every descriptive mention needs consent, and it does not claim
that neutral or internal naming automatically satisfies the terms.
