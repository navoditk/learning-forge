### Retrieval method and access limitations

CDE HTML pages under `www.cde.ca.gov` returned HTTP 200 to `curl` but the
body was a Radware bot-challenge ("Radware Captcha Page"), not the page.
**HTTP 200 on those pages is not treated as content retrieval.** The challenge
was not bypassed. Instead, official CDE PDFs were fetched directly (each
returned HTTP 200 with `application/pdf` and readable text extracted locally
with macOS PDFKit). Where only an HTML page holds a claim, a Wayback Machine
capture was used **as a secondary locator** and is labeled secondary.
Edition and date metadata below come from the documents' own front matter or
embedded PDF metadata, never from filenames.

### Standards framework, version, and California amendments

- **Governing standards**: _California Next Generation Science Standards_
  (CA NGSS), adopted by the SBE in September 2013 (primary, SCI6-S09 Ch. 1;
  exact day September 4, 2013 from secondary capture SCI6-S15), with Grade 6
  placement under the Preferred Integrated Model approved November 6, 2013
  (primary, SCI6-S07). Grade 6 text: SCI6-S01.
- **Amendments located**: California clarification-statement edits (SCI6-S04);
  the Grade 6 instance is MS-LS1-1 (adds Bacteria/Archaea/Eukarya and a viruses
  note). No later SBE amendment to the Grade 6 integrated PEs was located via
  these searches; the live CDE page could not be read to confirm.
- **Framework**: 2016 edition, adopted November 3, 2016, published June 2018
  (SCI6-S06). Guidance is non-binding. No newer adopted science framework was
  located; SCI6-S14 (April 2025) still lists 2016.
- **Architecture**: three-dimensional PEs combining SEPs, DCIs and CCCs; PEs
  are the assessable unit (SCI6-S02, SCI6-S03).

### Sequencing source and authority

No local pacing. The only sequencing input is the framework's non-binding
example storyline (SCI6-S07): guiding concept about organism systems adapted
to Earth's climate systems, in four segments — IS1 systems and subsystems in
Earth and life science; IS2 Earth-system interactions cause weather; IS3
regional climate causes and effects; IS4 effects of global warming on living
systems. The framework explicitly prescribes neither the order within a year
nor time per segment. Authoring may adopt this as a Learning Forge synthesis,
labeled "inspired by CDE framework example," and must also respect
prerequisite logic and assessment boundaries. The Grade 6 placement of PEs
(SCI6-S01) is mandatory; segment order is optional guidance.

### Content tiers, inquiry/lab inspiration, safety, and originality

- `core`: original phenomena, models, data sets and explanations for each of
  the 19 PEs within assessment boundaries; mastery evidence through structured
  items, never an LLM opinion alone.
- `depth`: original extended reasoning within the same PEs — multi-step
  modeling, claim-evidence-reasoning, comparing designs with data, California
  regional-climate contexts. Depth may go beyond assessment boundaries only if
  labeled non-assessed enrichment.
- **Pedagogy inspiration (style, not text)**: 3-D phenomenon-driven learning,
  5E cycle, problem-based learning, science/engineering notebooks and science
  talk (SCI6-S09 Ch. 11); UDL and access practices (SCI6-S09 Ch. 10); EP&C
  environmental framing (SCI6-S01, SCI6-S09 App. 2, SCI6-S17).
- **Inquiry and lab safety for an online Grade 6 product**: prefer provided
  original data sets, simulations reviewed for licensing and accessibility,
  observation of everyday phenomena, and thought experiments. Do not author
  hands-on procedures involving heat sources (framework vignettes use hot
  plates), flames, chemicals, sharp tools, electricity beyond sealed
  low-voltage devices, live animals, human-subject sensory testing, or outdoor
  hazards. Design challenges (MS-PS3-3, MS-ESS3-3, ETS1) should use
  paper/virtual prototypes or adult-supervised, school-led activities only.
  The CDE safety handbook (SCI6-S16) could not be read; obtain an accessible
  current safety authority before any hands-on activity is authored.
- **Originality and rights**: CDE framework is © 2018 CDE, all rights reserved
  (SCI6-S06); DCI text is NRC/NAP copyright (SCI6-S11). SCI6-S12 states two
  separate things: a public **copyright** permission to reproduce/adapt NGSS
  addressed to states, districts, schools, teachers and non-profits, and
  **trademark** guidelines (nonprofit and commercial) for the NGSS name and
  logo. Trademark guidelines are not a copyright license. Cite PE codes and
  write original short descriptors; do not reproduce or closely paraphrase
  framework vignettes, figures, tables, storyline wording, CAST items, EEI or
  PhET materials. No source is recorded as licensed for reuse. Do not use
  "NGSS" or the NGSS logo in a way that implies endorsement.
- **Age and access**: Grade 6 reading level, WCAG 2.2 AA, alt text for data
  visuals, no maps or charts that depend on color alone, and sensitive framing
  for climate-change content (agency and solutions, not alarm).

### Conflicts, gaps, and decisions requiring human review

1. **Live CDE HTML blocked.** Standards pages showed a bot challenge despite
   HTTP 200. Confirm, from an unblocked browser session, that no amendment
   newer than the documents above is posted.
2. **MS-ETS1-4 placement.** Standards document includes it in Grade 6; the
   framework's Grade 6 example bundles do not. Recommendation: include it in
   `core` (standards govern), with an explicit "6–8 band" note.
3. **Date conflict.** The archived CDE page says the Science Expert Panel
   reconvened in December 2013; Framework Chapter 5 and SCI6-S05 say
   December 2014. Neither affects Grade 6 placement; record both and do not
   assert one.
4. **SEP-5 and CCC-1** are not visible in Grade 6 foundation boxes as
   extracted. Decide whether depth content should deliberately include
   mathematical/computational and patterns practice per framework guidance.
5. **Safety authority unavailable.** The 2014 CDE handbook was not located.
   Approve the virtual/data-first, no-hazard inquiry policy above, or supply
   an alternative authoritative safety source.
6. **Rights questions, conditional only.** This packet proposes only PE-code
   references and original descriptors, with no reproduction of standards,
   framework or NRC text and no NGSS trademark/logo use; it adds no new legal
   gate. If reproduction of NGSS text is later proposed, whether Learning
   Forge is eligible under the public copyright permission becomes a question
   to resolve first. If use of the NGSS name/logo is later proposed, the
   trademark guidelines apply as a separate question. Nothing here grants
   permission to reproduce full standards or source text.
7. **Simulations/external resources.** PhET and EEI license terms were not
   verified; approve or reject before any learner-facing link or embed.
8. **Climate-content framing.** MS-ESS3-5 and IS4 require human-caused warming
   content; approve tone guidance for a Grade 6 learner.
9. **Mastery scope.** Several PEs require investigation or design performance
   that an online item cannot fully evidence. Decide which PE components are
   assessed in-product versus reported as "practiced, not assessed."
10. **No textbook/pacing.** Remains unspecified by choice; any later school
    textbook needs its own metadata verification and rights review.
