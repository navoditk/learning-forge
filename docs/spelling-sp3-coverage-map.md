## 10. Risks

- **R-1 Real-word recall versus pattern.** `sphere` and `knead` require
  recall of a real word's vowel spelling, not only the clue. That is the
  bee act, but a learner who has never seen the word cannot derive it. Both
  need the G-SOURCE check.
- **R-2 Uniqueness of invented spell-it items** rests on the stated
  constraints and on "regular English spelling" claims (no final j; initial
  kw is qu; final j after a short vowel is dge; a doubled consonant keeps a
  single vowel letter short). Each claim has loanword exceptions (for example
  `raj`, `kwanza`), which only the "regular English / not borrowed" wording
  excludes. Rev 2 now fixes every vowel letter in the six invented items:
  kimalo by the Pellan table, frodel as `o`, fredge and quendle as `e`,
  reblitting as `i`, and glunnier by the sunny/funny model. So the open
  choices are only the patterns each item tests. The harness still rejects
  only the wrong spellings the author listed.
- **R-3 C-1 strictness.** Accepted answers are only the exact option texts.
  "an animal", quoted answers or option letters are rejected. This is the
  C-1 rule as written, but it can mark a correct choice wrong.
- **R-4 Capitalized cues and assistive technology.** Screen readers may read
  a capitalized syllable (`SFEER`, `FREJ`) letter by letter. Stress is also
  stated in words, and the stored alternatives use lowercase, but
  alternatives for legacy `text` items are not projected today (scope row
  17). Needs the owner AT pass (G-A11Y).
- **R-5 Version coexistence.** Registration must decide whether each
  `content-2` replaces its `content-1` in the live list (D-344 forbids
  registration here). The harness loads both versions together; serving
  both would show a learner two versions of one item.
- **R-6 Shallow items.** Records 1, 2 and 11 are easy two- or three-option
  contest items; record 15 (`kimalo`) applies a fully supplied mapping.
  They are foundational-to-moderate evidence only.
- **R-7 Etymology facts** (`sphere`: Greek via Latin; `knead`: Old English)
  came from the author's knowledge. The coordinator relays that the Codex rev
  1 review verified both; the author retrieved no source.
- **R-8 Volume.** 8 of the 32 new records; the remaining six skills get no
  new records in this batch.
- **R-9 Hints not served.** Authored hints are stored, not shown (SP-4 is
  separate); their quality is unexercised by learners.
